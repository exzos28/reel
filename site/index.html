Pick a film. It plays in seconds.

reel is a source-available app with a torrent engine built in. It streams the film while it fetches it, so you never wait for a download. Made for the TV and the remote, and for phones, tablets and computers too.

reel comes with no content and no sources. It works with any search source that speaks the Torznab standard: Jackett, Prowlarr or any other. You connect your own.

What it does

Any Torznab source

reel speaks Torznab, the open search standard of Jackett, Prowlarr and other indexers. Add the address and key of any provider that answers in it; reel checks it and says how it searches.

Install on a Samsung TV
  1. On the TV, open Apps, press 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote and turn on Developer mode, with your computer's IP as Host PC IP. Restart the TV.
  2. On that computer, open Apps2Samsung and pick the TV.
  3. Install the reel .wgt package. Apps2Samsung signs it for your TV.

Open code, no accounts

The whole app is on GitHub: a Rust core with the torrent engine, and one interface for every platform. Its code is open to read, change and share for any noncommercial purpose under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0; commercial use is not allowed.

  • No reel account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking code.
  • Settings and watch progress stay on your device.

Who reel talks to

  • Your search source, at the address you entered.
  • TMDB, AniList and Jikan, for titles, posters and descriptions.
  • A public anime ID list on GitHub, to match anime between catalogs.
  • Other people sharing the film. As with any torrent app, they can see your IP address.

Questions

What is reel?

A video player with a torrent engine and a film and series catalog. You pick a title, it finds releases through your search source and streams the one you choose.

Does reel come with films or sources?

No. reel ships no content, no tracker addresses and no indexers. Releases come only from the search source you add in Settings.

What is a search source?

Any search service that answers in the Torznab format, the open standard of torrent indexers: Jackett or Prowlarr on your computer or home server, or any other Torznab-compatible provider. You give reel its address and API key; reel does not depend on any one of them.

Is there an official or recommended search source?

No. The reel project does not run, sell or recommend any search source, and takes no money from anyone who does. reel works with any service that speaks the Torznab standard, so a service may call itself compatible with reel; that does not make it part of reel, endorsed by it, or connected to it. What such a service searches, and whether you may use it, is between you and that service.

Can others use the reel name?

To say a service works with reel, yes. Not in a way that suggests it is official, endorsed by the project or part of it, and not with the reel logo.

What am I allowed to watch?

reel is a tool, like a browser or a torrent client. What you search for and play is your choice and your responsibility. Play only what you have the right to watch where you live.

Does it fill up my device?

No. reel streams and keeps only a buffer of the film, up to the size you set in Settings. It needs that much free space to start.

Where do titles, posters and descriptions come from?

From TMDB for films and series, and from AniList and Jikan for anime. If you build reel yourself, add your own free TMDB key; without one, reel lists only anime.

Why is it not in the App Store or Google Play?

The App Store does not accept torrent clients, and reel is not published to any store. Packages will be on GitHub Releases; Samsung TVs install them through Apps2Samsung.

Is it free?

Yes, for personal and other noncommercial use. There is no account, no subscription and no ads. Using reel or its code for commercial purposes is not allowed (PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0).
